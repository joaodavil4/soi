Common questions

How do I team up with a friend?

One person taps Team up on the quest list, the other taps Join a friend’s quest. Keep both phones close with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on, then accept the invitation.

How do I report or block someone?

In a shared quest, open Team up → Report or block.

How do I delete my data?

Go to the Me tab, then Safety & privacy → Delete my data.

Does Soi! need an account?

No. Your photos stay on your phone.

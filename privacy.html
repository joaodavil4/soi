Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Soi! is a photo-quest app for travelers. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there is no account, your photos stay on your phone, and we don’t sell data or track you.

What Soi! uses

InformationWhyWhere it goes
Precise locationTo find your city and suggest missions and weather where you areSent to Open-Meteo (a weather service) to get the weather. The last city is saved on your phone. It is not linked to you.
Photos you take for a questYour montage and albumStored on your phone only. If you share a quest, the photos are sent straight to your partner’s phone.
Display name (default “Traveler”)Shown to the person you share a quest withSent to their phone over the nearby connection
Mission reactions, preferences, block listRemembering your choicesYour phone only

Soi! has no advertising and no analytics. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Camera to take your quest photos.
  • Location (while using the app) to detect your city.
  • Local network / Bluetooth to pair with a friend’s phone nearby. Connections are encrypted and both people must accept.
  • Photos (add only) to save your collage to your library when you tap Save.

Shared quests

Two people can share one quest. Photos either person takes appear on both phones. Photos already on your partner’s phone stay there if you leave. If a future version adds cloud sync, this policy will be updated first and sync will only run with a signed-in user.

Report, block and delete

  • Report someone in a shared quest from Team up → Report or block. We review reports within 24 hours and act on abusive content.
  • Block someone from the same screen. This ends the shared quest and stops them from joining you again. You can unblock on the Me tab.
  • Delete my data on the Me tab erases your photos, montages, stamps, preferences and quest progress from your phone. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your phone.

Third-party services

Soi! asks Open-Meteo for the weather at your coordinates. Soi! also includes Google Firebase libraries; this version does not use them to store your photos, name or any account data.

Children

Soi! is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
